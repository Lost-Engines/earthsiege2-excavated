Earthsiege 2 was developed by Dynamix and published by Sierra On-Line. The original models, artwork, audio, text and other game content remain the work of their creators and respective rights holders. This site presents selected extracted content and screenshots as part of an independent reverse-engineering study.

The browser viewers and writing are part of the [Lost Engines Project](https://lostengines.com/). Original game assets are kept distinct from the site code. This repository does not include the game executables, disc images or a playable web port.

Kevin Foley's [Herculan](https://github.com/kevinfoley/Herculan) provided format and executable research used as leads and cross-checks. The retained [MIT notice](../research/HERCULAN-LICENSE.txt) accompanies those references. The earlier Earthsiege investigation and [Open Siege](https://github.com/open-siege/open-siege) informed the volume and 3Space readers.

The illustrations are captures of the original executable or the analysis viewers. Captions identify the source; no game screenshots were generated with an image model. The [source guide](../research/) and provenance manifests describe the inputs.
